Privacy policy

Grinch Drive Sync · last updated October 13, 2026

Who uses it

This app is used by one person, its owner, to reach their own Google Drive from their own home computers. Nobody else can sign in or use it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive. It uses Google's Drive API only for that.

Where the data goes

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computers. The app does not send Drive data to any other person, company or service, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
