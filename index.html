Grinch Drive Sync

A personal, single-user tool. Not a public service.

Grinch Drive Sync lets one person's home computer read and write that same person's own Google Drive files, so a private home dashboard can keep its notes in sync. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers.

What it does

  • Mounts the owner's Google Drive on the owner's home server.
  • Reads and writes only files in the owner's own Drive.

What it does not do

  • It does not offer sign-up to anyone else.
  • It does not sell, share or send Drive data to anyone.
  • It does not show ads or track visitors.
